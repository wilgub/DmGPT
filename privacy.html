Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-24

1. Information We Collect

When using the DmGPT Discord bot (“the Service”), the following limited information may be processed or stored:

  • Discord User ID
  • Server role or subscription tier information
  • Usage statistics such as message counts and token usage

Message content is processed temporarily only to generate responses requested by users. Message content is not permanently stored by the Service.

2. How Data Is Used

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Provide AI chat responses requested by users
  • Apply usage limits and subscription tiers
  • Prevent abuse and maintain service stability
  • Monitor system performance and usage

3. Message Processing

The bot only processes message content when users intentionally interact with it, such as through commands, replies, or direct messages. The Service does not monitor or analyze unrelated conversations in servers.

4. AI and Model Training

User messages are not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models operated by the Service owner.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Usage data may be stored for operational purposes such as enforcing limits and preventing abuse. Data may be deleted periodically or upon request where reasonably possible.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties except where required to operate the Service (for example, processing requests through AI providers).

7. User Rights

Users may request deletion of stored data associated with their Discord account by contacting the bot owner through the official support server.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact the bot owner via the official support server.
